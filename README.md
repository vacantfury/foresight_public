# AI Foresight

Dated, resolvable, probability-stamped forecasts about a world being transformed by frontier
AI — published so the record can be scored, not argued about.

**Current edition: v1 (2026-07-25), 26 claims (F1–F26).** First claim resolves **2026-08-31**.
The full edition, including the divergence table against tracked forecasting questions, is in
[`forecast.md`](forecast.md).

## What makes this a forecasting record rather than an essay

- **Every claim names its resolution source and threshold in advance** — a specific published
  number, market, or document on a named date. A claim that cannot be scored is not made.
- **Claims are never edited after their edition is stamped.** Revisions are new claims in a
  later edition; the old ones stay on the record and are Brier-scored when their dates arrive.
  The scoring rules and the resolution ledger are in [`scorecard.md`](scorecard.md) —
  published empty on purpose: the first entry is due **2026-08-31**, so the promise is on the
  record before any score is.
- **The outside view comes first.** Each claim starts from a measured trend or a tracked
  forecasting question (Metaculus, Polymarket, Manifold, Swift Centre), and every step away
  from that anchor is argued in writing. The divergence table at the top of the edition shows
  exactly where and by how much this record disagrees with the markets — the fastest feedback
  the system has.
- **Revisions use Bayesian odds updating** — priors as odds, both likelihoods written down and
  recorded in the edition, so a number never moves without its reasons on the record.

## Scope

Five slices — AI capability trajectory · knowledge-work labor market · immigration & US–China
climate · financial/economic · regulation & societal response — plus two far-tier (2032–2040)
structural priors. The slices were chosen for decision relevance to the author, which is the
point: these are forecasts someone acts on, not commentary.

## How to read a claim

Each claim carries: a probability and resolution date · the exact resolution test · the
anchors, with every number read from a fetched primary source at write time · the written
reasoning for any divergence from the anchor · what evidence would change the number.
Bracketed `W#` tags cite the thesis cards in [`worldview.md`](worldview.md) — the argument
layer beneath the claims, one card per mechanism, each carrying its strongest counterargument
and the observables that would move it. `WT#` tags cite a scenario-tree layer still in the
working repo; it is published after its next review round.

## Provenance

This repository is a generated publication surface: the work happens in a private working
repo, and each edition is rendered here on its revision cadence (next revision ~October
2026). Nothing here is edited by hand. Challenges to specific claims are welcome as issues —
disagreement with a number is exactly what the divergence table is for.
